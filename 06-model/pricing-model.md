# The Model · FinWise

> Module 6 · Pricing & Monetization. The pricing & packaging recommendation memo for FinWise.

## Stage + model

**Stage:** Stage 1 · Value Creation, Low retention because YoY churn is 60% and trial to paid conversion is only 2% which shows that Finwise should still prove its value to the end users

**Model:** Freemium, Finwise needs scale and habit before it can monetize, so trading some short-term revenue for adoption and engagement is the right call

_____

## Recommendation

Move new users from a free trial to a reverse-trial freemium model: every new user gets 14 days of premium, then drops to a usable free tier that gates the advanced training plans they've started relying on. If we can manage to provide sufficient value to the users in the 14 days, then they will convert to paid users

**The pricing bet:** Validating that the free/paid line sits right at the line where users feel they need more.
