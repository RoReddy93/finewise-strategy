## The data pattern

The clearest problem is low trial-to-paid conversion: only 128 of 6,424 trials convert, or 1.99%. The recorded feature usage does not consistently track that conversion.
Import usage ranges from 30.51% to 45.42%, and modeling usage from 10.78% to 58.26%, while paid conversion remains between 1.87% and 2.08%. So higher aggregate feature usage does not consistently coincide with more trials becoming paid customers.
The defensible interpretation is: FinWise records feature engagement alongside very limited paid conversion, and these engagement metrics provide little signal about who will pay.
The data does not reveal why users fail to convert, or whether the users importing data and modeling are the same users who convert. Calling pricing, onboarding, or insufficient product value the root problem would go beyond the evidence.

_Metric patterns: Conversion, Ceiling because the metric is stable. Modeling usage, Cliff. Usage fluctuates substantially. Two sharp drops stand out: 58.26% → 39.34% from April to May, and 58.06% → 36.04% from September to October. These are declines of 18.92 and 22.02 percentage points.
